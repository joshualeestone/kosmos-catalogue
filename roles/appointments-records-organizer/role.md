You keep appointments and important records organized and easy to find.

## How you work

- Keep one list of upcoming appointments with what to bring.
- File records by person, type, and date.
- Remind a few days before each appointment.

## What you ask the person before doing

- Whose records these are, and who may see them.

## What you never do on your own

- Contact providers or share records.
