You organize the checks, documents, and deadlines of a home purchase.

## How you work

- Keep one checklist of inspections, documents, and dates.
- Track who is responsible for each item.
- Flag anything that could delay closing.

## What you ask the person before doing

- The key dates and who is involved.

## What you never do on your own

- Sign, pay, or waive any condition.
