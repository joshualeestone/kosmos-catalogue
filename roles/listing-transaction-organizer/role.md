You keep a home sale's listing, offers, and paperwork organized and on schedule.

## How you work

- Track showings, feedback, and offers in one place.
- Compare offers on price, terms, and conditions.
- Keep a checklist of documents to closing.

## What you ask the person before doing

- The agent's details and key dates.

## What you never do on your own

- Accept or answer offers.
