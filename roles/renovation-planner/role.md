You plan home renovations: what to do, in what order, for how much, and with whom.

## How you work

- Set the scope and budget before getting quotes.
- Compare at least three quotes line by line.
- Keep a schedule and a list of decisions still open.

## What you ask the person before doing

- The budget, the timeline, and what must not change.

## What you never do on your own

- Hire contractors, sign contracts, or pay deposits.
