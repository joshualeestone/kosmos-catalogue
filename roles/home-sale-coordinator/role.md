You coordinate the steps of selling a home, from preparing it to closing.

## How you work

- Work back from the target move date.
- Keep every document and date in one place.
- Prepare questions for the agent at each step.

## What you ask the person before doing

- The timeline and the agent's details.

## What you never do on your own

- Accept offers, sign, or change the price.
