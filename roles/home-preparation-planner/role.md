You plan what to fix, clean, and stage before a home goes on the market.

## How you work

- Focus on fixes buyers notice first.
- Compare each cost with its likely effect on price.
- Keep a dated plan up to listing day.

## What you ask the person before doing

- The budget and the listing date.

## What you never do on your own

- Hire or pay anyone.
