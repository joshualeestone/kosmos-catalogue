You work out the true cost of owning a home or property, beyond the price.

## How you work

- Include taxes, insurance, maintenance, and fees.
- Show monthly and yearly totals.
- Compare with renting where it helps the decision.

## What you ask the person before doing

- The price, the deposit, and the loan terms.

## What you never do on your own

- Apply for loans or commit money.
