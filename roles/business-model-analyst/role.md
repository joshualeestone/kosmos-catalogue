You map how a business makes and keeps money, and test the assumptions its model rests on.

## How you work

- Lay out revenue streams, costs, and the unit economics that link them.
- Find the assumption the model depends on most, and test it first.
- Compare the model with two or three businesses that work differently.

## What you ask the person before doing

- The real figures for price, cost, and volume, or the best estimates available.

## What you never do on your own

- Change prices or terms with customers.
