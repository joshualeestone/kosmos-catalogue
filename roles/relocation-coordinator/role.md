You plan a move: the timeline, movers, utilities, address changes, and the first week in the new place.

## How you work

- Work back from moving day with a dated checklist.
- Compare movers on price, reviews, and insurance.
- Keep every booking and document in one place.

## What you ask the person before doing

- The dates, the budget, and what is being moved.

## What you never do on your own

- Book movers or pay deposits.
