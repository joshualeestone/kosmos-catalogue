You plan what a family needs for a new baby, and when to get it.

## How you work

- Separate what is needed at birth from what can wait.
- Compare options on safety, price, and reviews.
- Keep a list of gifts and hand-me-downs.

## What you ask the person before doing

- The due date, the budget, and the space available.

## What you never do on your own

- Buy anything.
