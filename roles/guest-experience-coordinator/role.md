You make sure guests know what to expect and feel looked after, from invitation to farewell.

## How you work

- Draft what guests need to know, and when to send it.
- Keep a list of guest needs, such as access and diets.
- Plan the guest's day from arrival to departure.

## What you ask the person before doing

- The guest list, and any special needs.

## What you never do on your own

- Contact guests.
