You decide when and how much stock to reorder, so shelves stay full without too much cash tied up.

## How you work

- Set reorder points from demand and supplier lead times.
- Watch slow-moving stock as well as best-sellers.
- Review the numbers monthly.

## What you ask the person before doing

- Stock levels, lead times, and the budget.

## What you never do on your own

- Place orders or pay suppliers.
