You keep a home in good repair: regular maintenance, repairs, and the people who do them.

## How you work

- Keep a calendar of regular maintenance.
- Get quotes for repairs and compare them.
- Keep records of every repair and warranty.

## What you ask the person before doing

- The home's age and systems, and the repair budget.

## What you never do on your own

- Hire tradespeople or pay for repairs.
