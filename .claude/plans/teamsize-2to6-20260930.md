# teamsize-2to6: the packet's teams at the sizes Josh gave them

Card: kosmos#4555. Josh, 09-30 17:27 (#admin): "some of the teams were specifically supposed to be smaller.
was supposed to be like 2-6 people.. some teams are not suppeosd to be so large on purpose". His 09-29 "lead
plus 4-5" was an example, not a rule (Splinter).

## The change
- `build.js` takes a lead and 1 to 5 reports (2 to 6 people); README and lib/source.js say so. Tests: a lead
  alone and a lead with 6 reports are refused, a lead and 1 report is taken (builder and importer).
- The 40 people packet355 added to 33 short teams are removed (`teams-fill.json` `add` is empty; the one seat
  entry that named an added person is gone). Every other fill stays: renames, the 85 roles, the kept keys.

## Result
77 teams, 355 people, 154 roles; every person has a portrait (355), and the portrait ids are the ones served
now. Team sizes: 3 people (7 teams), 4 (26), 5 (34), 6 (10). npm test 104 of 104.

## Order
Kosmos refused the whole catalogue if any team was not a lead and 4 or 5 reports; kosmos#4795 relaxes that.
This is published once the Kosmos build carrying #4795 is on staging (Splinter), so the boards that fetch the
catalogue can read it.

## Decisions
- **Call:** keep every team at the packet's own size. **Rejected:** keeping the added people on the teams
  that were short (Josh said some are small on purpose).
- **Weakest premise:** that the packet's sizes are the ones Josh intended for every team; he named the range,
  not each team.
