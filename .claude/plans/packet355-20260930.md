# packet355: the 09-29 teams packet into the catalogue (kosmos#4555, #4632)

Josh's packet (`teams-packet-demis-1524`: 77 teams, 355 people, 171 roles) goes in without a regenerated
packet (Splinter's call, 2026-09-30 15:00). The gaps are filled here, as data in `packet355-fills/`,
applied to a copy of the packet by `packet355-fills/apply.js`, then imported by `tools/import-packet.js`.

Dry run on main 9e250ae before the fills: 732 refusals (355 presentations, 165 first actions counted
twice, 33 short teams, 14 names).

## What was filled, and why

1. **presentation (all 355).** The packet put a personality phrase there. It carries how each person
   presents in another way: hair and attire come from two disjoint sets of six lines, and every member
   is in exactly one (177 and 178). Those sets became `woman` and `man`. Checked against Josh's
   portraits: 24 of 24 randomly chosen portraits (seed 4555) show what the set says.
2. **The 165 new roles: summary, first action, opening sentence, and the instructions.** The packet has
   only 12 distinct instruction sets across 171 roles; 120 roles share one operations set word for
   word, and every summary is one template ("Focuses on <name> responsibilities..."). #4555 asks for
   instructions good enough to use unedited, so each role has its own three How-you-work rules, and
   ask-first and never-alone lines (`roles-1.json` to `roles-7.json`). The packet's archetypes are kept.
3. **Cautions.** The packet had 26, in five stock sentences, and none on some roles that send, book,
   pay or advise on money (payroll, booking, reviews, investment research). Each role now has a short
   caution where the brief's rule calls for one, in the published style: 41 of 165 (24.8%).
4. **33 short teams to a lead plus 4:** 40 new people, each on an existing role, a heritage not already
   on the team, a presentation that balances the team, and looks drawn from the packet's own sets.
   Blurbs say the new count; "A Automation Lead" and the like read "An".
5. **Names.** 14 packet people shared a first name with a published team; they are renamed (same
   heritage and presentation; titles unchanged, so each still maps to Josh's portrait). Every name is
   checked against the published catalogue, the packet, and kosmos's no-name-refs-3071 list, and by
   the builder's port of Kosmos's name rule (which refuses accents: two first choices were refused).
6. **Portraits.** Josh's 355 (`~/Documents/avatars-512`), as Baron's WebP copies (512 px, metadata
   stripped, 15 to 37 KB, SHA256SUMS checked: 355 of 355). Named `<team>-<slot>` by
   `packet355-fills/portrait-map.txt`, generated from the imported slots: 355 mapped, none left over,
   no id twice. The 40 new people have no portrait yet.
7. **Test fixture.** `check-deploy passes exactly the signed build` built the real catalogue but wrote
   only its own one portrait, so with real portraits in `avatars/` the control failed ("avatars/
   marketing-lead.webp is missing"). The fixture now clears image fields first; the check is unchanged.

## Result

`node build.js`: 234 roles and 91 teams (469 members), 355 with a portrait. `npm test`: 99 of 99.
Seven published teams are replaced by the packet's (content, sales, marketing, website, operations,
finance, family): the packet is the newer spec for the same subjects.

## Decisions

- **Call:** fill and import now. **Rejected:** waiting for a regenerated packet (no date, and Josh asked
  for the list today); importing roles first and teams later (the importer writes only when nothing
  is refused, and teams need their roles).
- **Weakest premise:** that the hair and attire sets encode presentation. A perfect split plus a 24/24
  portrait sample, but no document says so.
- **Not done:** portraits for the 40 new people (Baron, #4720); the packet's personality blocks are not
  in the catalogue format (the importer has never carried them).
